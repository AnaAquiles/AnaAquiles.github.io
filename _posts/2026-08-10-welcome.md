This is the first mark on a blank page — the start of a space I'll fill slowly, with research, tools I've come to trust, and reflections too long for a tweet and too alive to stay in a drawer. Some of it will echo what I used to post on Medium; the difference is that now, this corner is entirely mine.

A few things to expect here, in no particular order:

- Notes scribbled in the margins of papers I'm reading or writing
- Diaries of side projects, the ones that grow quietly on weekends
- Opinions — sometimes strong, sometimes still forming — on tools, workflows, and the strange world of academia

Thanks for stopping by.
