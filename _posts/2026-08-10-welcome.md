This is the first mark on a blank page _the start of a space_ I'll fill slowly, with research, tools I've come to trust, and reflections too long for a tweet and too alive to stay in a drawer. Some of it I used to just mull over in my head, or drop into a voice note for my friends (special thanks to Cindy, for playing all those voice notes). The difference now is that I'll share it with you instead.

A few things to expect here, in no particular order:

- Notes scribbled in the margins of papers I'm reading or writing
- Diaries of side projects, the ones that grow quietly on weekends
- Opinions *sometimes strong, sometimes still forming* not necessarily based on scientific knowledge, but on philosophy, music and the mundane aspects of daily life. 

_Thanks for stopping by!_
